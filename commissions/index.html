Commissions

Misha accepts a limited number of commissions each year: original abstract paintings made for a particular space, palette or scale, in the same layered acrylic, ink and collage language as her series work.

How it works

  • Tell Misha about the space (photos help), preferred size, colours and timing.
  • She proposes a size, approach and price, with a small number of reference works from her portfolio.
  • A deposit secures a place in the studio schedule; the balance is due on completion.
  • You see progress images before the work is finished and shipped or delivered.

Typical lead time is 6–10 weeks depending on size.
