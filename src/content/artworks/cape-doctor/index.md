From the Trade Winds series, palms on paper. Each painting is titled after a real named wind. The palms are bent, blurred and interrupted by movement, so the true subject is the air rather than the tree: invisible forces made visible. Cape Town's summer south-easter, so called because it scrubs the air clean. The bluest of the winds. Sold framed (19¾ × 19¾ in).
