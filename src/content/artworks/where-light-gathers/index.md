This work explores invisible wave structures: the frequencies of sound, light, water, and energy that shape our physical world but remain unseen. I work in acrylic, ink, and spray paint on a fiber-optic light canvas, building translucent, layered gestures that move between emergence and dissolution. Fiber-optic light is woven into the canvas itself, so illumination becomes part of the painting and dissolves the boundary between light and painted surface. Unlit, it reads as a painting; lit, a second image emerges from within it. Sold framed: 42.5 × 57 × 3.5 in framed (canvas 40 × 55 in).
