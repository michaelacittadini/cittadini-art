In this series, I'm further exploring how waves impact us-both from nature and from people-shape our world. I want to capture how positive and negative vibrations move through today's geopolitical and environmental landscape. At this moment, more alarming waves are emerging. Even a single wave, from one person or one event, can impact the entire globe. This painting is my way of expressing those reverberations. I hope the positive wave will prevail in the future.
