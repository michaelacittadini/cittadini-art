In The Wave, the physical power of the ocean meets the invisible frequencies of sound and light. Part of the Resonance Series, this large-scale work uses a dynamic blend of acrylic, ink, and spray paint to visualize the idea that energy is a continuous, vibrating force connecting everything. Through sweeping, gestural arcs and misty atmospheric layers, the painting captures the rhythmic pulse of the universe, turning the unseen vibrations of our world into a powerful, visible presence.
