1st Place Abstract Award Winner at AAU Spring Show 2025
This painting captures the connection between sound, noise, and movement, much like music flowing through space. Just as the ocean holds all sounds (White Noise), this piece reflects layers of rhythm, echoes, and vibrations. The fluid shapes and dynamic contrasts evoke the energy of music - its waves of sound crashing, blending, and resonating like water in motion.
