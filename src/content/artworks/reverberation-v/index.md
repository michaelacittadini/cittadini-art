This piece is created with acrylics, inks, collage, and resin on wood. The layers of movement and contrast aim to capture a dynamic interplay of energy and structure, inviting the viewer to immerse themselves in its depth. It features four layers of resin, with each layer painted to enhance the sense of depth.
