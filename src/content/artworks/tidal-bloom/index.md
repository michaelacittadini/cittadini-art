From the Interference series. In physics, interference is what happens when waves meet and overlap: they reinforce and cancel, creating a pattern neither wave holds alone. Here it happens literally — finished painted canvas is cut apart and its translucent pieces are layered and glued onto raw canvas, so that something new appears where they cross.
