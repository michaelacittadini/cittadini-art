Radiant Frequency

Year
2026
Medium
Acrylic, Ink, Spray Paint on Fiber-Optic Light Canvas
Dimensions
53 × 39 × 1.8 in
Series
Secret Light
Availability
Available — $8,500

This work explores invisible wave structures: the frequencies of sound, light, water, and energy that shape our physical world but remain unseen. I work in acrylic, ink, and spray paint on a fiber-optic light canvas, building translucent, layered gestures that move between emergence and dissolution. Fiber-optic light is woven into the canvas itself, so illumination becomes part of the painting and dissolves the boundary between light and painted surface. Unlit, it reads as a painting; lit, a second image emerges from within it.
