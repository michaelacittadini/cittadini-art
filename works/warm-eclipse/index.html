Warm Eclipse

Dimensions
24 × 24 × 1.8 in

Availability

From the Interference series. In physics, interference is what happens when waves meet and overlap: they reinforce and cancel, creating a pattern neither wave holds alone. Here it happens literally — finished painted canvas is cut apart and its translucent pieces are layered and glued onto raw canvas, so that something new appears where they cross. Warm Eclipse is the first work in the series.
